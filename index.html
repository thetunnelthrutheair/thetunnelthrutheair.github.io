Panchang details
Rahu, Gulika and Yamaganda divide sunrise–sunset into eight parts; Choghadiya uses eight day and eight night parts; Abhijit is the 8th of the 15 day muhurtas; Brahma Muhurta is 96–48 minutes before sunrise. Varjyam and Amrit Kalam use the traditional per-nakshatra tables. Vikram Samvat is Chaitra-based.
